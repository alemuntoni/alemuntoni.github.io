The tone mapping operators available when rendering.

Enumerator
NONE 

No tone mapping is applied.

BASIC 

Basic (Reinhard) tone mapping.

ACES_HILL 

ACES Filmic Tone Mapping (Hill).

ACES_HILL_EXPOSURE_BOOST 

ACES Filmic Tone Mapping (Hill) with pre-exposure boost.

ACES_NARKOWICZ
